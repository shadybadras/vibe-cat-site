Vibe Cat Support

There's a cat in your phone, and sometimes it needs help. Here's how to get the most out of it.

The cat doesn't react to my music

  • Make sure the microphone is allowed: Settings › Vibe Cat › Microphone.
  • Play the music out loud from a speaker, laptop or another phone. Headphones keep the sound away from the mic.
  • Songs with a clear beat work best. Give it a few seconds to find the tempo.

The cat doesn't move when I move my phone

  • Allow Motion & Fitness: Settings › Vibe Cat › Motion & Fitness.
  • Try a higher Sensitivity in the gear menu.

How do I make it purr?

Swing your phone side to side so each change of direction lands on the beat. Keep it up for a few seconds and the cat closes its eyes and purrs.

How do I record a clip?

Tap the round button at the top left and allow screen recording. From then on, tapping it shares the last 10 seconds. If the cat gets knocked over while recording, a “Share that” button appears for the fall.

I bought something and it's missing

Open the gear menu, scroll to Extras and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Can I try a cat before buying?

Yes. Tap any locked cat to try it free for a minute. You can try each cat once a day.

Why is it glaring at me?

You shoved it out of the screen. It will forgive you… eventually.
