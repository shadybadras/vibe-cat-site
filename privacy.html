Vibe Cat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Vibe Cat collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app itself sends nothing over the internet (purchases go through Apple).

Microphone

Vibe Cat uses the microphone only to hear the beat of music playing around you, so the cat can move with it. The sound is analysed live on your device and immediately discarded. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Motion

Vibe Cat reads your phone's motion sensor so the cat moves when you move your phone. This data is used only on your device, in the moment, and is not stored or shared.

Clips

When you tap the record button, iOS keeps the last few seconds of the screen and microphone ready on your device so you can share them. A clip is only saved when you tap to share it or when the cat gets knocked over while recording is on, and it stays on your phone unless you choose to share it. Vibe Cat never uploads clips anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Vibe Cat only learns which items you've unlocked; it never sees your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Settings

Your chosen cat, scene, outfit and sensitivity are saved on your device so they're there next time. They never leave your phone, and deleting the app removes them.

Children

Vibe Cat is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
